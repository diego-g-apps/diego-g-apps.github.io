In short

  • You do not need to create an account or give us your name, email or phone number.
  • What you save in the app (such as your settings) stays only on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google collects some device data, such as the advertising ID.
  • As the developer, I do not receive, sell or store your personal data on my own servers.

1Who is responsible

This policy applies to the Android app Ruleta de Limpieza, distributed on Google Play. Developer and data controller: Diego G. Contact: ahlanenergy@gmail.com.

2Data the app stores on your device

To work, the app stores the following in your phone's private storage:

  • The list of chores you type in for the wheel.
  • Your sound and music settings.

This data is not sent to the developer or anyone else. You can delete it at any time from Android Settings › Apps › Ruleta de Limpieza › Storage › Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

3Advertising: Google AdMob

The app shows a banner at the top of the screen using Google's advertising SDK (Google Mobile Ads) and Google's consent platform (User Messaging Platform). Google LLC, or Google Ireland Limited if you are in the European Economic Area or Switzerland, processes this data under its own policies.

To serve ads, the Google SDK may automatically collect and send to Google:

  • Your Android advertising ID and App Set ID.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) can be inferred. The app does not use GPS.
  • Technical device information: model, Android version, language and connection type.
  • Interactions with ads (for example, impressions and clicks) and SDK diagnostic and performance data.

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse and, only if you agree or if the law in your region allows it, to show you personalised ads.

Your consent and how to change it

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows you a Google consent form before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time with the “Privacidad de anuncios” (ad privacy) button on the settings screen (it appears when your region requires it).

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (usually under Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads) and manage your ad preferences in Google My Ad Center.

4Android permissions

The version reviewed declares these permissions:

PermissionWhat it is for
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATELoading ads and checking whether there is a connection.
AD_IDLets the Google SDK read the advertising ID.
READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATEAdded by the included Google libraries. It gives access to basic, non-sensitive phone information (such as the network type). The app does not read your phone number, calls or contacts.
WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICEUsed internally by Android and Google components included in the app for their technical tasks. The app has no background services of its own.

The app does not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

5What I do not do

  • I do not require sign-up or a user account.
  • I do not use my own analytics or statistics services (for example, Firebase Analytics).
  • I do not sell or rent personal data.
  • I have no servers of my own where user data is stored.

6Who data is shared with

Only with Google, through AdMob, as explained in the “Advertising” section. I do not share data with anyone else.

7Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone: kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Data processed by Google: Google keeps it according to its privacy policy. You can delete or reset your advertising ID at any time.
  • Emails you send me: I keep them only as long as needed to reply, and then delete them. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

The app has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete.

8Security

The data the app saves is kept in its private storage, which other apps cannot access. Communications between the Google SDK and its servers are encrypted.

9Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (for example, the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, to request its portability and to withdraw your consent at any time. Since I do not keep any of your data (except emails you send me), for advertising data you can use the app's consent options and Google's tools. For any request, write to me at ahlanenergy@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

10Children

The app does not ask for the age, name or any data that identifies the person using it. Ads are served by Google AdMob under its policies. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent me personal data by email, write to me and I will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

If a new version of the app changes how data is used (for example, by adding ads, permissions or services), I will update this page and the date above.
