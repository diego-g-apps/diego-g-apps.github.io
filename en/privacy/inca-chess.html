In short

  • You do not need to create an account or give us your name, email or phone number.
  • The app has no internet permission: it sends no data off your phone.
  • It shows no ads and includes no analytics or third-party services.
  • What you save in the app stays only on your phone.

This app is in development. This policy describes the version shown above; if a future version adds ads, internet access or other features, I will update it before publishing.

1Who is responsible

This policy applies to the Android app Inca Chess, distributed on Google Play. Developer and data controller: Diego G. Contact: ahlanenergy@gmail.com.

2Data the app stores on your device

To work, the app stores the following in your phone's private storage:

  • The game you save to continue later.
  • Your preferences, such as the chosen board.
  • A local profile with a randomly generated player name and your game history. It never leaves your phone.

This data is not sent to the developer or anyone else. You can delete it at any time from Android Settings › Apps › Inca Chess › Storage › Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

3Android permissions

The version reviewed declares no permissions at all: no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

4What I do not do

  • I do not require sign-up or a user account.
  • I do not show ads.
  • I do not use my own analytics or statistics services (for example, Firebase Analytics).
  • I do not sell or rent personal data.
  • I have no servers of my own where user data is stored.

5Who data is shared with

No one: the app sends no data off your phone.

6Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone: kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Emails you send me: I keep them only as long as needed to reply, and then delete them. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

The app has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete.

7Security

The data the app saves is kept in its private storage, which other apps cannot access.

8Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (for example, the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, to request its portability and to withdraw your consent at any time. Since I do not keep any of your data (except emails you send me), for advertising data you can use the app's consent options and Google's tools. For any request, write to me at ahlanenergy@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

9Children

The app does not ask for the age, name or any data that identifies the person using it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent me personal data by email, write to me and I will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

If a new version of the app changes how data is used (for example, by adding ads, permissions or services), I will update this page and the date above.
